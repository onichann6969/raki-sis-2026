Happy Raksha Bandhan

to my sister, Sahana

From Devang

This is just a small message for someone who has genuinely been one of the best people in my life.

I've never really had the privilege of having a blood-related sister — someone I could protect, annoy, laugh with, and make memories with. But somehow, in less than a year, you've managed to become all of that and so much more. You've been both an amazing protector and, more importantly, one of my best friends.

You've been there through some of the lowest and highest moments of my life. Whenever my mind starts overthinking, especially during those late nights when I have a million things going through my head, I know I can always come to you. You listen, you understand, and somehow you always manage to make things feel a little easier.

And honestly, some of my favourite memories are the simplest ones. Walking together after tuitions, eating samosas, talking about absolutely everything, and making the most ridiculous Malayalam cuss word jokes imaginable. They might seem like small moments, but they're the kind of memories I know I'll always cherish.

So, on this Raksha Bandhan, I just want to say thank you. Thank you for being there, for listening to me, for putting up with my nonsense and help me heal alot of my scars , and for becoming someone I can genuinely call my sister.

I promise that from now on, I'll always be there for you too. I'll protect you, support you, annoy you when necessary, and stand by you through whatever comes your way.

I love you. ❤️
Happy Raksha Bandhan, Sahana. ❤️
